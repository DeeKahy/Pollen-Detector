\chapter{Interview guide: people with pollen allergy}\label{app:interview-guide}
% Draft by Claude, edit freely. Used in Section~\ref{sec:allergy-interviews}.

\fillin{Draft. Test the questions on one person before the real interviews.}

About 30 minutes. The questions are a guide, not a script. Ask follow-up questions such as
``Can you tell me about the last time that happened?''.

\section*{Before the interview}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Explain the project in two sentences, and that there are no right or wrong answers.
  \item Ask for consent to record. Explain that names are not used and recordings are deleted
  once the notes are written.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Questions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item How does your pollen allergy affect you? Which time of year is worst?
  \item Where do you get information about pollen today (app, website, TV, none)?
  \item How often do you check it during the season? Can you tell me about the last time?
  \item What do you do differently when the count is high?
  \item Does the official count match how you feel where you live? Can you give an example?
  \item Would it help to know the count for your own area instead of for all of East or West
  Denmark? How close would it need to be (your street, your town, your region)?
  \item How often would you want new numbers (once a day, every hour)?
  \item Would you trust a number from a cheap station that a neighbour built? What would make
  you trust it more or less?
  \item Would you build or host a station yourself? What would stop you (price, time, skills,
  space)?
  \item Is there anything about pollen information you wish existed?
\end{enumerate}

\section*{After the interview}
Thank the participant. Write down the main points the same day.

\chapter{Written questions to Astma-Allergi Danmark}\label{app:expert-questions}
% Draft by Claude, edit freely. Used in Section~\ref{sec:expert-email}.

\fillin{Draft. Translate to Danish before sending? Keep it short so it gets answered.}

\textit{Short introduction: who we are, the project in two sentences, a link to the repository,
and that they are welcome to answer only some of the questions.}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item How is the daily count made today, and roughly how many working hours does it take each
  week?
  \item What limits the number of stations in Denmark (price of the trap, staff, something
  else)?
  \item How accurate does a count need to be to help a forecast? Is a daily count enough, or
  would hourly counts help?
  \item Which pollen types matter most for the Danish forecasts?
  \item What would you need to see before you could trust counts from a cheap automated station?
  \item Would a network of cheap stations be useful to you? Who do you think should look after
  them?
  \item Would you be open to placing one of our stations next to one of your traps to compare the
  counts?
\end{enumerate}
